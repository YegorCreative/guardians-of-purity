Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Policy

Guardians of Purity was designed to provide a safe, discreet experience. Your journey here is private — and we aim to keep it that way with minimal data collection and maximum care.

Effective: May 2025

Our Approach

Privacy by Design

Guardians of Purity was designed to provide a safe, discreet experience. This is a space for healing — and healing requires trust. We believe the less data we hold about you, the better.

Most of our content is freely accessible without creating an account or sharing any personal information. When you do choose to reach out, every detail is handled with care and confidentiality.

We aim to collect as little information as possible.
Most content is accessible without creating an account.
We do not sell personal information.
No public profiles. No exposure. No judgment.
You are safe here. Guardians of Purity was designed to be a private, discreet space. Your journey belongs to you alone.
Transparency

Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little information as possible. Most of our content is freely accessible without providing any personal details. Here is a clear picture of what may be collected and why:

Information You Provide

You are never required to create an account to read our content. If you choose to reach out or fill in a form, you may share your name — but it is entirely optional.

Email Address

We only ask for your email if you choose to subscribe or contact us. We will never email you without your permission, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Prayer Requests

Prayer requests are held in the strictest confidence. They are never stored publicly, never shared, and used only to pray and respond to you directly.

Usage Analytics

Aggregated, anonymized data about how pages are visited helps us improve the platform experience for the entire community.

Device Information

Basic technical data such as browser type, operating system, and screen size may be collected to ensure the site functions correctly on all devices.

Cookies & Sessions

Small session files may be stored in your browser to preserve your progress and preferences. No personally identifiable data is stored in cookies.

Purpose

How We Use Your Information

Whatever little information we do collect is used only to serve you — never to track you, profile you, or turn you into a product.

1

Supporting You

To respond to your messages, prayer requests, and support inquiries with care and timeliness.

2

Improving the Platform

To understand how the website is used so we can fix issues and deliver a better experience for everyone.

3

Sending Encouragement

If you opt in to our mailing list, we may send updates, devotionals, and new resources to your inbox.

4

Security & Fraud Prevention

To monitor for abuse, prevent spam, and keep the platform safe for everyone in the community.

5

Legal Obligations

In the rare case of a legally required request, we respond with only the minimum necessary information and always with full care for your privacy.

Commitment

How We Protect Your Data

We take practical steps to keep your information secure and private. Here is what that looks like in practice:

We do not sell personal information — not to advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else.
We aim to collect only the minimum information needed to serve you.
Access to your information is strictly limited to authorized personnel.
Sensitive data is handled using secure, encrypted channels.
We regularly review our practices to maintain strong data hygiene.
No unnecessary data is retained beyond its useful purpose.
Prayer requests are never publicly shared or disclosed.
While we strive to protect your data, no system can guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to use strong passwords and to contact us immediately if you suspect any unauthorized access.
Partners

Third-Party Services

Guardians of Purity uses a small, intentionally minimal set of third-party services. We choose tools with strong privacy practices and never use them to advertise, retarget, or profile you.

  • Hosting Providers — Our website is hosted on secure, industry-standard infrastructure. Hosting providers may access server logs in the normal course of operation.
  • Analytics Services — We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand traffic patterns and improve the user experience. Data is aggregated and anonymized.
  • Email Delivery — Contact form submissions and newsletter signups are processed by secure email delivery services. Your email is used solely to communicate with you.
  • Web Fonts & CDN — We use Google Fonts and content delivery networks to load the site quickly. These services may set standard technical cookies.
These services are operational tools, not data partners. Your information is never used for advertising, retargeting, or profiling of any kind.
You Are in Control

Your Rights

We believe you should always be in control of your own information. Here is what you can request at any time:

  • Access — Request a copy of any personal information we hold about you.
  • Deletion — Ask us to delete your data. We will honor this request promptly unless we are legally required to retain it.
  • Correction — Request that we update or correct inaccurate information we hold about you.
  • Opt-Out — Unsubscribe from communications at any time using the link in any email we send, or by contacting us directly.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@guardiansofpurity.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Safeguarding

Children's Privacy

Guardians of Purity is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@guardiansofpurity.com and we will take immediate steps to remove that information.

Staying Current

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we do, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of Guardians of Purity following any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.

Questions About Privacy?

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests about this Privacy Policy, our team is here to help. You are not alone — we take every inquiry seriously.
