Chapter 28: Reigniting Your Passion for Holiness

“Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they will be filled.”
— Matthew 5:6

Passion for holiness is not created from nothing; it is reignited when we return to the source and tend what God has already placed within us.

Parable: The Rekindled Fire

There was a man who kept a fire burning in his home.

At first, the flames were strong and warm, lighting the entire room. But over time, he grew busy and stopped tending it. The fire did not go out—it faded into glowing embers, giving just enough heat to survive, but no light to fill the space.

One evening, he knelt beside the hearth and placed fresh wood onto the embers. He blew gently, not forcing the flame, but giving it air. Slowly, the fire caught again, rising brighter than before.

The warmth returned—not because the fire was new, but because it had been cared for.

Moral:
Passion for holiness is not created from nothing; it is reignited when we return to the source and tend what God has already placed within us.

1. Introduction: Rediscovering Your Hunger for God

Spiritual numbness often diminishes your passion for holiness, making it hard to desire righteousness. This chapter explores practical and spiritual ways to reignite your hunger for God and pursue holiness with renewed zeal.

2. Why Do We Lose Passion?

Understanding why passion fades helps you respond with wisdom and grace.

  • Habitual Sin: Persistent sin suppresses the desire for God and holiness.
  • Disconnection from God: Neglecting prayer, worship, and Scripture leads to spiritual stagnation.
  • Burnout or Overload: Overwhelming schedules can crowd out time for spiritual growth.

3. Reigniting Your Hunger for God

God welcomes honest prayers for renewed desire. Small steps toward Him can reignite passion.

  • Pray for Desire: Even if you don’t feel like praying, ask God to give you a hunger for Him.
  • Scripture: “Restore to me the joy of Your salvation and grant me a willing spirit, to sustain me.” — Psalm 51:12
  • Revisit the Gospel: Reflect on God’s grace and the sacrifice of Jesus.
  • Engage in Worship: Worship music and corporate worship can stir your heart and remind you of God’s glory.

4. Daily Practices to Stay Passionate

Daily habits keep your heart engaged and your focus steady.

  • Morning Devotion: Start each day with Scripture and prayer, asking God to guide your steps.
  • Small Acts of Obedience: Build momentum by making small, consistent choices that honor God.
  • Gratitude Journal: Record blessings to keep your focus on God’s goodness.

5. Overcoming Barriers to Holiness

Barriers can be removed when you name them and build healthy rhythms.

  • Break the Cycle of Distraction: Identify and remove time-wasters or unhealthy habits.
  • Rebuild Healthy Routines: Create rhythms of prayer, worship, and Scripture study that support growth.
  • Lean on God’s Strength: Holiness is not achieved by willpower alone; it requires dependence on the Holy Spirit.

6. Reflection Questions

Use these questions to help you take practical steps toward renewed passion.

Questions to Reflect On

  1. What areas of your life have become spiritually stagnant?
  2. How can you reignite your passion for God through worship or Scripture?
  3. What small changes can you make today to move closer to holiness?

You've Completed Chapter 28

Well done. God can rekindle what has grown faint. Keep returning to Him, and tend the small daily practices that restore your hunger for righteousness.

Chapter Summary

  • Passion can fade through sin, neglect, and overload.
  • God can restore desire and spiritual hunger.
  • Worship, Scripture, and the gospel renew the heart.
  • Small daily obedience builds lasting momentum.
  • Healthy routines protect long-term holiness.
Final Reflection: What is one daily practice you will start this week to rekindle your hunger for God?

Closing Prayer

Heavenly Father, Thank You for calling me to live a holy life. I confess that my passion for You has waned at times. Please rekindle my hunger for Your Word, Your presence, and Your righteousness. Help me to stay focused on You and to live each day with intentionality and joy. Guide me in making choices that reflect Your holiness. In Jesus’ name, Amen.
