What to Do When Your Body Is Naturally Erected

"But I discipline my body and keep it under control, lest after preaching to others I myself should be disqualified."

(1 Corinthians 9:27)

Natural responses are not your enemy. Wisdom is learning how to guide them.

Parable: The Young Horse and the Strong Wind

In a wide green valley lived a young horse named Arin.

Arin was strong and full of energy. Sometimes, without warning, a powerful wind would rush across the valley. When it did, Arin felt his legs grow restless and his body surge with strength.

He would begin running wildly across the field, unsure what to do with the sudden energy inside him.

One day, after running in circles until he was exhausted, Arin walked over to an older horse named Soren who calmly grazed near the hill.

"Why does this happen to me?" Arin asked. "The wind comes, and suddenly my body wants to run. I cannot stop it."

Soren lifted his head and looked kindly at the young horse.

"The wind is not your enemy," he said. "It is simply part of the valley."

Arin tilted his head.

"But what should I do when it comes?" he asked.

Soren pointed toward the hills in the distance.

"When the wind rises," he said, "run toward something that matters."

So the next time the wind swept across the valley, Arin felt the same rush of energy in his body.

But instead of running in circles, he ran toward the hill.

Day after day he grew stronger, learning how to guide his strength instead of fearing it.

And soon the wind that once confused him became the very thing that helped him grow.

Moral:
Natural strength is not wrong. Wisdom is learning how to guide it.

Introduction: Understanding Natural Responses

Erections are a natural part of being male and a sign of your body functioning as God designed. They often occur without conscious thought or intent, especially during puberty or times of hormonal fluctuation. This chapter explains how to handle natural erections in a way that aligns with your faith and respects God's design.

1. Why Erections Happen

  • Physiological Responses: Erections occur due to increased blood flow to the penis, triggered by various factors like physical stimulation, hormonal activity, or even during sleep (known as nocturnal penile tumescence).
  • Not Always Sexual: Erections can happen without sexual thoughts or desires. They are part of your body's natural function. For example, many young men experience morning erections due to hormonal activity during sleep cycles.

2. How to Respond to Natural Erections

When your body reacts naturally, here are steps to manage the situation without letting it lead to temptation:

  • Shift Your Focus: Distract yourself by engaging in an activity or focusing on something non-sexual, such as a hobby or task.
  • Pray for Strength: Ask God to help you maintain self-control and purity in your thoughts. "Set your minds on things above, not on earthly things." (Colossians 3:2)
  • Avoid Feeding Temptation: Refrain from dwelling on thoughts that may escalate sexual desires.

3. The Importance of Self-Control

  • Discipline Your Body: Self-control is a fruit of the Spirit and essential for managing your body in a God-honoring way. "For the Spirit God gave us does not make us timid, but gives us power, love, and self-discipline." (2 Timothy 1:7)
  • Recognize the Moment: Acknowledge that your body's reaction is natural, but choose not to let it lead to sinful thoughts or actions.

Do This Now

Memorize 1 Corinthians 9:27 and repeat it during moments when you need to exercise self-control.

Questions to Reflect On

  • How can self-control help you manage natural bodily responses?
  • What does it mean to discipline your body in alignment with God's Word?
  • How can practicing self-discipline in small moments prepare you for bigger challenges?
  • What role does the Holy Spirit play in strengthening your self-control?

4. Using These Moments for Spiritual Growth

Natural erections can serve as reminders to turn to God for strength and to focus on His design for your body.

  • Refocus on God's Purpose: Use these moments as opportunities to pray, meditate on Scripture, or refocus on your daily goals. "So whether you eat or drink or whatever you do, do it all for the glory of God." (1 Corinthians 10:31)
  • Practice Gratitude: Thank God for your body and its ability to function as He designed.

Do This Now

The next time you feel distracted by a natural bodily response, take a moment to pray and thank God for His design.

Questions to Reflect On

  • How can these moments remind you of God's purpose for your body?
  • What Scriptures encourage you to focus on God instead of distractions?
  • How does gratitude change the way you view natural bodily functions?
  • How can these experiences strengthen your faith and reliance on God?

5. Seek Accountability and Guidance

If you find it difficult to handle natural erections without letting them lead to temptation, seek support.

  • Talk to a Mentor or Trusted Friend: Sharing your struggles with someone you trust can provide encouragement and accountability.
  • Be Honest with God: Bring your thoughts and struggles to God in prayer. He is faithful to guide you.

Do This Now

Identify someone you trust who can provide accountability. Commit to reaching out to them when you need support.

Questions to Reflect On

  • How can accountability help you handle challenges?
  • What steps can you take to remain honest with God and yourself?
  • How does sharing struggles strengthen your faith community?

Chapter Summary

  • Erections are a natural physiological response and not always linked to sexual intent.
  • You can respond with wisdom by shifting focus, praying, and setting clear boundaries.
  • Self-control is Spirit-empowered and grows through daily discipline.
  • These moments can become opportunities for gratitude and spiritual growth.
  • Accountability and honest prayer strengthen long-term purity and resilience.
