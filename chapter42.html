Running to God, Not From Him

“How can a young person stay on the path of purity? By living according to Your word.”

— Psalm 119:9

Practical, actionable steps to stay on the path of purity.

Parable: The Footpath and the Lantern

There was once a young man who lived near a wide field that led to a beautiful hilltop chapel. Each day he told himself, “I want to be there,” but he never chose a clear route. Sometimes he walked at night. Sometimes he wandered without direction. And more than once, he found himself standing at the edge of a swampy patch where the ground looked safe—until it wasn’t.

One evening, an older shepherd saw him covered in mud and said, “You keep trying to arrive at a holy place with unholy steps.”

The young man frowned. “I’m trying. I just keep slipping.”

The shepherd nodded and handed him three things: a map, a lantern, and a whistle.

“The map is your plan,” the shepherd said. “It shows you where not to walk and where to go instead.”

“The lantern is the Word of God. It doesn’t remove the darkness, but it shows the next right step.”

“And the whistle,” he smiled, “is what you use when you feel yourself drifting. One sound, and help comes running.”

The young man began to walk again—this time with boundaries, light, and support. He still felt the pull of old paths, but now he recognized them early. When the mud called his name, he checked the map. When shadows pressed in, he raised the lantern. And when he felt weak, he blew the whistle and someone answered.

Over time, the path became clearer—not because the field changed, but because he did.

Moral:
Purity isn’t only a desire—it’s a direction. A plan, God’s Word, and accountability turn good intentions into steady steps.

Introduction: Pursuing a Life of Purity

Living a life of purity requires intentionality, discipline, and reliance on God’s strength. Purity is not just about avoiding sin; it is about aligning your thoughts, desires, and actions with God’s will. This chapter provides practical, actionable steps to help you stay on the path of purity in your daily life.

Do This Now

Write one sentence about why purity matters to you right now, and ask God for strength as you apply these steps.

Questions to Reflect On

  • Why does purity matter to you right now?
  • Where do you feel most vulnerable to temptation?
  • What would consistent progress look like this week?
  • What is one step you can take today?

1. Develop a Plan for Purity

  • Set Clear Boundaries: Decide in advance what you will avoid and where you will draw the line.
  • “But Daniel resolved not to defile himself with the royal food and wine.” (Daniel 1:8)
  • Establish Accountability: Find a trusted mentor, friend, or accountability partner who can encourage and challenge you.
  • Have a Plan for Triggers: Identify situations or emotions that tempt you and create strategies to respond in a God-honoring way.

Do This Now

Write down one boundary you need to establish and share it with your accountability partner.

Questions to Reflect On

  • What boundaries can you set to protect your purity?
  • Who can hold you accountable in your journey?
  • What triggers temptations, and how can you prepare for them?
  • How does planning ahead help you pursue purity?

2. Fill Your Mind with God’s Word

  • Meditate on Scripture: God’s Word is a powerful tool for resisting temptation and renewing your mind.
  • “I have hidden Your word in my heart that I might not sin against You.” (Psalm 119:11)
  • Replace Negative Thoughts: Focus on what is true, noble, and praiseworthy (Philippians 4:8). Replace impure thoughts with the promises and truths found in Scripture.

Do This Now

Memorize one verse that addresses purity or self-control. Repeat it daily to strengthen your mind.

Questions to Reflect On

  • How often do you meditate on God’s Word?
  • What Scriptures encourage you in moments of temptation?
  • How can you replace negative or impure thoughts with God’s truth?
  • How can Scripture equip you to pursue purity?

3. Stay Busy with God-Honoring Activities

  • Avoid Idleness: Boredom often leads to temptation. Fill your time with productive, meaningful activities.
  • “A slack hand causes poverty, but the hand of the diligent makes rich.” (Proverbs 10:4)
  • Pursue Your Passions: Channel your energy into hobbies, volunteering, or creative projects that honor God. Serving others can refocus your mind away from temptation.

Do This Now

List three activities or hobbies you can pursue when you feel idle or tempted.

Questions to Reflect On

  • How can staying busy protect you from temptation?
  • What activities bring you joy while glorifying God?
  • How can serving others help you focus on what truly matters?
  • What steps can you take to avoid idleness in your daily routine?

4. Practice Gratitude and Prayer

  • Focus on God’s Blessings: Gratitude shifts your focus from what you lack to what you have in Christ.
  • “Give thanks in all circumstances; for this is God’s will for you in Christ Jesus.” (1 Thessalonians 5:18)
  • Pray Without Ceasing: Regular prayer helps you stay connected to God and rely on His strength to overcome temptation.

Do This Now

Start a gratitude journal. Write down three things you’re thankful for each day, focusing on God’s goodness and faithfulness.

Questions to Reflect On

  • How does gratitude help you fight temptation?
  • How can prayer strengthen your pursuit of purity?
  • What blessings can you thank God for today?
  • How can focusing on God’s goodness transform your thoughts?

5. Replace Temptation with Healthy Relationships

  • Surround Yourself with Godly Friends: Choose friends who encourage your faith and help you stay accountable.
  • “Walk with the wise and become wise, for a companion of fools suffers harm.” (Proverbs 13:20)
  • Build a Support System: Stay connected to your church, small group, or mentor to receive encouragement and guidance.

Do This Now

Identify one person in your life who strengthens your walk with God. Reach out to them this week for support and encouragement.

Questions to Reflect On

  • How do your relationships influence your pursuit of purity?
  • Who in your life encourages your faith and growth?
  • How can you strengthen your support system?
  • What steps can you take to avoid negative influences?

6. Trust in God’s Strength

  • God’s Power in Weakness: You cannot overcome temptation on your own. Trust in God’s strength to carry you through.
  • “But He said to me, ‘My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness.’” (2 Corinthians 12:9)
  • Remember God’s Faithfulness: He always provides a way out of temptation.
  • “No temptation has overtaken you except what is common to mankind. And God is faithful; He will not let you be tempted beyond what you can bear.” (1 Corinthians 10:13)

Do This Now

Spend time in prayer, asking God for His strength to overcome specific struggles. Trust in His faithfulness to help you.

Questions to Reflect On

  • How can you rely more on God’s strength in moments of temptation?
  • What does it mean for God’s grace to be sufficient for you?
  • How has God been faithful in helping you overcome struggles in the past?
  • How can you trust Him more fully in your pursuit of purity?

Chapter Summary

  • Purity requires intentional planning and boundaries.
  • God’s Word renews your mind and strengthens your resolve.
  • Staying busy with purpose helps reduce temptation.
  • Gratitude and prayer keep your heart connected to God.
  • Healthy relationships support long-term purity.
  • Victory comes through relying on God’s strength.
