Common Questions

Common Support Topics

Practical answers to what most people ask before reaching out.

How do I start using the site?

Visit the Journey page and begin with Chapter 1. There is no registration, no setup, and no barrier to entry. Read at your own pace — the platform is designed so you can return to exactly where you left off using your browser’s bookmarks or the built-in progress system.

Do I need an account?

No. Every chapter, prayer resource, and tool on Guardians of Purity is accessible without creating an account or providing personal information. Your privacy is protected by design — not just by policy.

Is my activity private?

Yes. Guardians of Purity does not track your reading history, does not display your activity publicly, and does not sell data to third parties. For full details on how data is handled, read the Privacy Policy.

I found a broken link or bug. What should I do?

Please contact us and describe what you found — the page name, the broken element, and what you expected to happen. Reports like these genuinely help us improve the platform for everyone. You do not need to write a detailed report — a short description is enough.

Where do I find prayer resources?

The Prayer page has guided prayers organized by moment and need — for when you feel tempted, after a setback, or when words are difficult to find. You can also find thematic prayers embedded throughout the Journey chapters.

Is this a replacement for counseling?

No. Guardians of Purity is an educational and faith-based resource — not a clinical service. It is designed to support, not replace, professional counseling or therapy. If you are dealing with trauma, crisis, or complex mental health needs, we encourage you to seek qualified professional help alongside using this platform.

Mobile App

App Support

The Guardians of Purity app is built to bring faith-based recovery content to your device — privately, simply, and without requiring an account. It is privacy-conscious and designed to be low on data usage.

App Not Loading

Try closing and reopening the app, checking your internet connection, or reinstalling. If the issue persists, contact us.

Content Display Issue

If a chapter or page is not rendering correctly, please note the page and report it. Include your device type if possible.

Navigation Issue

If you cannot reach a section or a link is unresponsive, please describe what happened and send a report.

Privacy Question

If you have a question about what data the app collects or stores, read our Privacy Policy or ask directly.

Feedback or Suggestion

We genuinely want to hear what would make this platform more helpful for you. Send your thoughts — they are read and considered.

Important Safety Note

Guardians of Purity is educational and faith-based support. It is not emergency care, crisis counseling, or a replacement for professional mental health treatment. If you or someone you know is in crisis, please contact a qualified professional or a crisis helpline immediately. We care about your wellbeing — and that means being honest about what we are and what we are not.

For urgent mental health support in the United States, you can contact the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline by calling or texting 988.

Still Need Help?

If you could not find what you need, you can reach out without explaining everything perfectly. We are here.
