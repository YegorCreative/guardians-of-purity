31 DAYS OF PRAYER FOR PURITY

Start Your Journey to Freedom — One Prayer at a Time

Commit the next 31 days to prayer, reflection, and victory in Christ.

Why 31 Days of Prayer?

We believe lasting freedom from sexual sin doesn’t come through willpower alone — it comes through intimacy with God. These 31 days are an intentional invitation to seek the Lord daily, not with shame, but with boldness, trust, and surrender. Prayer is not just a tool — it’s a weapon in the battle for your purity.

As you commit each day to God, you’ll build spiritual muscle, renew your mind, and break down the strongholds that keep you bound. This is more than a devotional — it's a journey of reformation. You won’t walk this path perfectly, but every prayer is a step closer to who God has called you to be: pure, whole, and free.

“So if the Son sets you free, you will be free indeed.”

— John 8:36

This is not about perfection, but pursuit.

You don’t need to have it all together. You just need to start. Show up each day, even if it’s messy. God will meet you in the struggle, and His strength will be made perfect in your weakness.

DAY 1

Pray for God’s Presence

Verse: “The Lord is near to all who call on Him, to all who call on Him in truth.”
(Psalm 145:18)

Prayer: Lord, draw near to my child. Let them feel Your presence and know that You are with them in every moment, even in their struggles. Surround them with Your love and peace.
Amen

DAY 2

Pray for Conviction and Repentance

Verse: “If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to purify us from all unrighteousness.”
(1 John 1:9)

Prayer: Father, I pray for gentle conviction to lead my child to repentance. Let them understand the depth of Your forgiveness and feel encouraged to turn back to You wholeheartedly.
Amen

DAY 3

Pray for a Renewed Mind

Verse: “Do not conform to the pattern of this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind.”
(Romans 12:2)

Prayer: Lord, renew my child’s mind with Your Word. Replace thoughts of temptation with thoughts of truth, purity, and peace. Help them to focus on things that glorify You.

Amen

DAY 4

Pray for Freedom from Strongholds

Verse: “The weapons we fight with are not the weapons of the world. They have divine power to demolish strongholds.”
(2 Corinthians 10:4)

Prayer: Father, demolish any stronghold of pornography or masturbation in my child’s life. Break the chains that hold them captive and replace them with Your strength and freedom.
Amen

DAY 5

Pray for Purity

“Blessed are the pure in heart, for they will see God.”
(Matthew 5:8)

Prayer: Lord, create in my child a pure heart. Let their thoughts, desires, and actions be aligned with Your will. Guide them to pursue purity in all areas of their life.

Amen

(Ecclesiastes 4:9)
