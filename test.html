clip-path

Clipping method 1

Preferable when the horizontal stretch of the angled columns (relative to column width) remains constant and not the angle. This does not require trigonometry nor container queries, but it requires fraction calculations.

clip-path

Clipping method 2

But it can also be used when the angle remains constant. This requires container queries and trigonometry in addition to fraction calculations, as well as nesting img elements in figure wrappers.

transform

Skewing method

Preferable when the angle remains constant. Requires container queries and trigonometric functions, as well as nesting img elements in figure wrappers, so we can un-skew the actual images after their containers get skewed.
